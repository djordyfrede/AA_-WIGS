Frequently Asked Questions

13×6 Swiss HD Lace is the thinnest lace available — virtually invisible against all skin tones with no visible lace line. The larger frontal area gives you maximum parting freedom, and the Swiss HD material creates an undetectable hairline that appears to grow directly from the scalp. Read our full HD Lace Guide →
With proper care, AA WIGS units are designed to last 1–2+ years. Every unit is made with 100% virgin human hair and sealed wefts, built to maintain their texture and beauty with correct maintenance.
The most popular lengths at AA WIGS are 22" and 24", which offer a glamorous, flowing look. If you prefer something more manageable for daily wear, 18"–20" is ideal. See our full Length Guide for a detailed breakdown →
Use a sulfate-free shampoo, detangle gently from ends to roots, and air dry when possible. Avoid excessive heat styling. Store on a wig stand to preserve the wave pattern. Full instructions are in our Wig Care Guide →
