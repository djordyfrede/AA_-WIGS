Quick Answers

Common Questions

How long does shipping take?

Processing takes 1–3 business days. U.S. delivery is typically 3–7 business days after that. Free shipping on every order — no minimums.

Can the wig be dyed?

Yes. Every AA WIGS unit is 100% virgin human hair and can be professionally dyed or color-treated, just like natural hair. We recommend working with a professional stylist.

What is your return policy?

14-day returns on unworn units in original packaging. Inspect your wig on arrival using our First Ten Minutes guide — it documents the condition before you install, which protects your return options.

How do I know the quality is real?

Every claim is documented. Our Buying With Confidence hub explains how we inspect every unit, what virgin human hair actually means, how our photography is produced, and what you should check on arrival.
