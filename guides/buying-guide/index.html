Human Hair Wig Buying Guide

Everything you need to know to choose the perfect human hair wig — from lace types and lengths to density and styling options.

Why Choose a Human Hair Wig?

Human hair wigs offer the most natural look and feel of any wig type. Unlike synthetic alternatives, luxury human hair wigs can be styled with heat tools, dyed to your preferred shade, and last significantly longer with proper care. Whether you're looking for everyday confidence or a special-occasion transformation, human hair wigs deliver unmatched versatility and realism.

At AA WIGS, our units are crafted from 100% virgin human hair — meaning the cuticles are intact and aligned in one direction. This prevents tangling, maintains a natural shine, and ensures your wig moves and feels like your own hair.

Understanding Wig Types

Not all wigs are created equal. Here are the most common types and what sets them apart:

Lace Front Wigs

Lace front wigs feature a sheer lace panel along the front hairline, creating a seamless, natural-looking transition between the wig and your skin. The lace allows you to part the hair naturally and style it away from the face. A 13x6 lace frontal — like the one used in our Signature Body Wave — provides extra parting space and versatility.

Full Lace Wigs

Full lace wigs have a lace base that covers the entire head, allowing you to part the hair in any direction and even pull it into updos. They offer maximum styling flexibility but tend to be more expensive and delicate.

Glueless Wigs

Glueless wigs are designed to be worn without adhesive. They feature adjustable bands, combs, or elastic straps inside the cap for a secure fit. Glueless human hair wigs are ideal for beginners and anyone who wants a quick, damage-free install. Our AA Signature Body Wave is fully glueless with an adjustable band for all-day comfort.

Body Wave Human Hair Wigs

Body wave is one of the most popular wig textures. It features soft, flowing S-shaped waves that add volume and movement without being overly curly. Body wave human hair wigs are low-maintenance and versatile — they can be straightened for a sleek look or curled for more definition.

HD Lace vs. Regular Lace: What's the Difference?

The lace type is one of the most important factors in how natural your wig looks. Here's how they compare:

Swiss HD Lace

HD (High Definition) lace is an ultra-thin, virtually invisible lace material that blends seamlessly with all skin tones. Swiss HD lace wigs offer the most undetectable hairline possible — the lace literally melts into your skin when applied correctly. This is the lace type used in all AA WIGS units.

Why we chose Swiss HD lace: It's thinner than standard lace, more breathable, and requires minimal customization to achieve a flawless, natural hairline. Learn more in our HD Lace Wig Guide.

Regular Lace

Standard lace is thicker and may require tinting to match your skin tone. While more durable, it's easier to detect and doesn't provide the same invisible finish as HD lace. For anyone prioritizing a natural look, HD lace is the superior choice.

How to Choose the Right Wig Length

Wig length affects your overall look, maintenance level, and price. Here's a quick breakdown of the most popular lengths:

  1. 18 inches — Falls around the collarbone. A versatile, everyday length that's easy to manage and style. Great for first-time wig wearers.
  2. 20 inches — Reaches just past the collarbone. Offers more length without feeling heavy. A popular choice for a balanced, natural look.
  3. 22 inches — Our most popular length. Falls mid-chest and provides the perfect balance of drama and manageability. Ideal for body wave texture.
  4. 24 inches — Reaches the lower chest. A statement length that adds volume and movement. Perfect for special occasions.
  5. 26 inches — Falls at or below the waist. Maximum drama and impact. Best for those who love long, flowing hair.

Not sure which length is right for you? Check our detailed Wig Length Guide for visual comparisons and recommendations based on your height and lifestyle.

Understanding Wig Density

Density refers to how much hair is on the wig cap. It's measured as a percentage — the higher the number, the fuller the wig.

  1. 130% density — Light and natural. Mimics the look of average natural hair thickness.
  2. 150% density — Medium fullness. A good balance between natural and voluminous.
  3. 180% density — Full and luxurious. This is the density used in all AA WIGS units. It provides a rich, voluminous look without appearing overly thick.
  4. 200%+ density — Very full and dramatic. Can feel heavier and may require more styling to look natural.

We chose 180% density for our Signature Body Wave because it strikes the ideal balance — full enough to turn heads, but natural enough for everyday wear.

Pre-Plucked Hairline: Why It Matters

A pre-plucked hairline means the hair along the lace has been carefully thinned to mimic a natural hairline. Without pre-plucking, a wig can look unnaturally thick and dense along the front — a dead giveaway that you're wearing a wig.

All AA WIGS units come pre-plucked with baby hairs for a ready-to-wear natural hairline. No additional customization needed.

What to Look for in a Quality Human Hair Wig

When investing in a luxury human hair wig, here are the key quality indicators to check:

  1. Hair quality — Look for 100% virgin human hair with intact, aligned cuticles. This prevents tangling and ensures long-lasting softness.
  2. Lace quality — Swiss HD lace is the gold standard. It should be thin, breathable, and virtually invisible against your skin.
  3. Construction — Check for reinforced wefts, secure knots, and a well-made cap. Bleached knots make the parting look more natural.
  4. Fit system — A quality glueless wig should have adjustable straps and combs for a secure, comfortable fit without adhesive.
  5. Packaging and presentation — Premium brands invest in packaging that protects the wig during shipping and enhances the unboxing experience.

The AA WIGS difference: Every unit ships in a luxury magnetic box with burgundy exterior, champagne satin lining, care guide card, and protective satin storage bag.

Glueless vs. Glue-Down Installation

One of the biggest decisions when choosing a wig is how you'll install it. Here's a comparison:

Glueless Installation

Glueless wigs use adjustable bands, combs, and elastic straps to stay in place. Benefits include:

  1. No adhesive damage to your natural hair or skin
  2. Quick and easy to put on and remove
  3. Beginner-friendly — no special skills required
  4. Ideal for daily wear and active lifestyles

Glue-Down Installation

Some wearers prefer using adhesive (wig glue or tape) for a more secure, flat-lying lace. This method can provide an even more seamless look but requires more time, skill, and product.

Our recommendation: Start glueless. Our Swiss HD lace wig already blends seamlessly without adhesive. If you want to try glue later, use a gentle formula designed specifically for HD lace.

How to Care for Your Investment

A high-quality human hair wig can last 1–2 years or longer with proper care. The basics include:

  1. Wash every 7–10 wears with sulfate-free shampoo
  2. Deep condition every two weeks to maintain softness
  3. Store on a wig stand to preserve shape
  4. Limit heat styling and always use a heat protectant
  5. Handle the lace gently — it's the most delicate part
