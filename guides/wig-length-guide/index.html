Wig Length Guide: 18" to 26"

Choosing the right wig length can transform your entire look. Use this guide to find your perfect body wave wig length.

Why Wig Length Matters

When shopping for a body wave wig or any human hair wig, length is one of the most important decisions you'll make. The right length complements your face shape, matches your lifestyle, and gives you the confidence to step out feeling your best. Whether you're new to wigs or a seasoned wearer, understanding how each length looks and falls will help you choose with certainty.

At AA WIGS, our Signature Body Wave collection is available in five lengths — 18", 20", 22", 24", and 26" — so you can find exactly the look you want.

How Wig Lengths Are Measured

Wig length is measured when the hair is pulled straight, from the root at the crown to the longest point at the tips. Because body wave wigs have a natural wave pattern, the hair will appear slightly shorter when worn due to the curl and bounce. A 22 inch body wave wig, for example, will typically fall a few inches shorter than 22 inches because of the wave texture.

Pro tip: If you want longer-looking hair, consider going one size up. For example, if you want hair that falls to your mid-back, a 24" body wave may be ideal since the wave shortens the appearance by 1–2 inches.

18" Body Wave Wig

Where It Falls

An 18 inch body wave wig typically falls just past the shoulders to the upper chest area. When straightened, the hair reaches approximately armpit length.

Best For

This length is perfect for anyone who wants a natural, everyday look. It's lightweight, easy to manage, and works beautifully for professional settings. If you're new to wearing human hair wigs, 18" is an excellent starting point — it looks effortlessly natural without requiring a lot of maintenance.

Styling Notes

At 18 inches, you have enough length to create loose curls, a half-up style, or a sleek low ponytail. The body wave pattern adds movement and dimension without excess weight.

If you want a balance between length and ease of styling, 20" is the sweet spot. It's long enough to make a statement but not so long that it requires extra care. This is one of the most popular lengths for body wave human hair wigs because of its versatility.

Styling Notes

The 20" length gives you room for side parts, curtain bangs, high ponytails, and voluminous blowout styles. The body wave texture looks especially stunning at this length, offering a glamorous yet natural appearance.

How to Choose the Right Wig Length for Your Face Shape

While every length can work for any face shape, certain lengths tend to be especially flattering:

  1. Round face: Longer lengths like 22"–26" help elongate the face and create a slimming effect. A middle part with a body wave wig adds vertical lines that balance round features.
  2. Oval face: You can pull off any length beautifully. Oval faces are the most versatile — try 20" or 22" for a universally flattering look.
  3. Heart-shaped face: Lengths that fall past the chin — 18" and longer — add width at the bottom and balance a narrower chin. Body wave texture is especially flattering.
  4. Square face: Soft, wavy textures like body wave help soften angular features. Lengths of 20"–24" work beautifully, especially with a side part.
  5. Long face: Shorter lengths like 18"–20" add width and prevent the face from looking overly elongated. The body wave pattern adds volume at the sides.

Tips for First-Time Wig Buyers

If you're purchasing your first human hair wig, here are a few things to keep in mind when choosing your length:

  1. Start with 18"–22" if you're unsure. These lengths are the most manageable and look natural on most people.
  2. Consider your lifestyle. If you're active or prefer low-maintenance styling, shorter lengths are easier to care for.
  3. Think about where the hair falls on your body. Your height affects how each length looks — a 22" wig will fall differently on someone who is 5'2" versus someone who is 5'8".
  4. Remember the wave factor. Body wave wigs appear 1–2 inches shorter than their measured length due to the curl pattern.
  5. Invest in quality. A premium human hair wig with Swiss HD lace and proper density will look more natural at any length.
