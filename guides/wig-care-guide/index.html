How to Wash & Maintain a Human Hair Wig

Your complete wig care guide — from washing and conditioning to styling, storage, and long-term human hair wig maintenance.

Why Proper Human Hair Wig Care Matters

A high-quality human hair wig is an investment. Unlike synthetic alternatives, human hair wigs can be washed, styled, and treated just like your natural hair — but they require the right care routine to maintain their softness, shine, and longevity. Whether you own a body wave wig, a straight unit, or a curly style, this wig care guide covers everything you need to know about human hair wig maintenance.

With consistent care, AA WIGS units are designed to last 1–2+ years — looking salon-fresh every time you wear it.

How to Wash a Human Hair Wig: Step-by-Step

Knowing how to wash a human hair wig correctly is the foundation of any wig care routine. Washing removes product build-up, oils, and environmental residue while restoring the hair's natural bounce and movement.

When to Wash

Wash your human hair wig every 7–10 wears. If you use styling products frequently, you may need to wash more often. Signs it's time include dullness, tangling, or a heavy feel.

What You'll Need

  • Sulfate-free, paraben-free shampoo
  • Moisturizing conditioner (silicone-free recommended)
  • Wide-tooth comb or detangling brush
  • Microfiber towel
  • Wig stand or mannequin head
  • Leave-in conditioner or argan oil (optional)

Step-by-Step Washing Process

  1. Detangle first. Before wetting your wig, gently comb through with a wide-tooth comb. Start at the ends and work upward to the roots. This prevents matting when the hair is wet.
  2. Rinse with lukewarm water. Hold the wig under lukewarm running water, letting it flow in one direction — from root to tip. Never use hot water, as it damages the cuticle layer and can loosen ventilation knots.
  3. Apply sulfate-free shampoo. Work the shampoo gently through the hair in a downward motion. Avoid rubbing, twisting, or bunching the hair together. Focus on the cap area where oils accumulate.
  4. Rinse thoroughly. Remove all shampoo residue under lukewarm water. Leftover product causes build-up and weighs down the hair.
  5. Apply conditioner mid-length to ends. Avoid applying conditioner directly on the lace or cap, as this can loosen the knots. Leave it on for 5–10 minutes for deep moisture penetration.
  6. Rinse with cool water. A cool water rinse seals the hair cuticle, locking in moisture and enhancing shine.
  7. Pat dry with a microfiber towel. Gently squeeze out excess water. Never wring, twist, or rub the hair — this causes friction damage and tangling.
  8. Air dry on a wig stand. Place the wig on a stand or mannequin head and allow it to dry naturally. Avoid blow-drying unless absolutely necessary, and always use a cool or low-heat setting.

Pro tip: After drying, apply a small amount of argan oil or leave-in conditioner to the ends. This restores softness and adds a natural-looking sheen to your human hair wig.

Deep Conditioning for Human Hair Wigs

Deep conditioning is essential for maintaining the moisture balance of your human hair wig, especially if you heat-style regularly or live in a dry climate.

  1. Choose the right product. Use a deep conditioning mask or treatment designed for human hair wigs. Look for ingredients like keratin, shea butter, or coconut oil.
  2. Apply after shampooing. Work the treatment through damp hair from mid-length to ends, avoiding the lace and cap area.
  3. Leave on for 15–30 minutes. For extra penetration, cover the wig with a plastic cap. You can also apply gentle warmth using a hooded dryer on a low setting.
  4. Rinse with cool water. Remove all product residue and allow the wig to air dry on a stand.

Deep condition your human hair wig every 2 weeks for optimal results. This keeps the hair supple, reduces frizz, and prevents dryness that leads to breakage.

Detangling Your Human Hair Wig

Proper detangling prevents shedding, preserves the wave or curl pattern, and extends the overall life of your wig. Rough handling is one of the most common causes of premature wig damage.

  1. Always detangle before washing. Wet tangling causes more breakage than dry detangling when done correctly.
  2. Use the right tools. A wide-tooth comb or a wig-specific detangling brush is ideal. Avoid fine-tooth combs or regular brushes.
  3. Start from the ends. Work in small sections, gently combing from the tips upward to the roots. Never pull from the top down.
  4. Use a detangling spray. A lightweight detangling spray or leave-in conditioner makes the process easier and reduces friction on the hair shaft.
  5. Finger-comb daily. Between washes, finger-combing is the gentlest way to maintain your wig. It preserves the body wave pattern and minimizes shedding.

Pro tip: If you own a body wave human hair wig, finger-combing helps maintain the natural wave texture better than brushing.

Heat Styling a Human Hair Wig Safely

One of the biggest advantages of a human hair wig is the ability to heat style — straighten, curl, or create any look you want. However, excessive heat is one of the primary causes of wig damage.

Heat Styling Best Practices

  1. Always apply a heat protectant. Spray evenly through the hair before using any hot tools. This creates a barrier that reduces moisture loss and cuticle damage.
  2. Keep temperatures moderate. Stay at or below 180°C (350°F) for regular styling. The maximum safe temperature for most human hair wigs is 200°C (392°F), but lower is always better.
  3. Avoid the lace. Never apply heat directly to the lace frontal. This can warp, melt, or permanently damage the lace base.
  4. Limit frequency. Heat-style your wig no more than 2–3 times per week. The less heat you use, the longer your wig will last.
  5. Try heatless alternatives. Flexi rods, braids, bantu knots, and roller sets can create beautiful styles without any heat damage.

Restoring the Body Wave Pattern

If you've straightened your body wave wig and want to restore the original wave pattern, wet the hair thoroughly and braid it into 2–4 loose braids overnight. Allow it to air dry completely before unbraiding for bouncy, defined waves.

How to Store a Human Hair Wig

Proper storage is just as important as washing and styling when it comes to human hair wig maintenance. Poor storage leads to tangling, shape loss, and premature wear.

  1. Use a wig stand or mannequin head. This maintains the shape of the cap and prevents the hair from getting crushed or tangled.
  2. Loosely braid or wrap before storing. For longer wigs (20" and above), loosely braiding the hair prevents tangling during storage.
  3. Keep away from sunlight. Direct UV exposure causes color fading and dries out the hair cuticle. Store in a cool, dark, dry location.
  4. Use a satin or silk bag for travel. When you're on the go, a satin storage bag protects the wig from friction and dust. Your AA WIGS order includes a protective satin bag for this purpose.
  5. Avoid humid environments. Excess moisture can cause mildew or odor. If you live in a humid climate, consider adding a silica gel packet near your storage area.

Pro tip: Sleeping in your wig? Use a silk or satin pillowcase to reduce friction and preserve the style overnight.

HD Lace Care & Maintenance

The lace frontal is the most delicate component of your wig. Proper lace care ensures an invisible, natural-looking hairline every time you install.

  1. Cut with precision. Use sharp, fine-tipped scissors to trim the lace along your natural hairline. Take your time — once cut, lace cannot be repaired.
  2. Choose lace-safe adhesives. If you use glue or tape, opt for formulas specifically designed for HD lace. Harsh adhesives can deteriorate the lace over time.
  3. Remove adhesive gently. Use a lace-safe solvent to dissolve adhesive residue. Never pull, peel, or scrape — this tears the delicate lace mesh.
  4. Clean the lace after each wear. Gently wipe the lace area with a damp cloth or lace cleanser to remove oils and adhesive residue, keeping the lace clear and re-applicable.

Common Wig Care Mistakes to Avoid

Even with the best intentions, some common habits can shorten the life of your human hair wig. Here are the top mistakes to avoid:

  • Using hot water. Hot water damages the cuticle and loosens ventilation knots. Always use lukewarm or cool water.
  • Sleeping in your wig without protection. Friction from cotton pillowcases causes tangling and matting. Always use a silk or satin pillowcase or remove the wig before bed.
  • Over-washing. Washing too frequently strips the hair of its natural oils. Stick to every 7–10 wears unless product build-up is excessive.
  • Brushing when wet. Wet human hair is more fragile. Always detangle before washing, not after.
  • Applying conditioner to the roots or lace. This loosens the knots and causes shedding. Apply from mid-length to ends only.
  • Using products with sulfates or alcohol. These ingredients dry out the hair and accelerate damage. Choose wig-specific or sulfate-free hair products.
  • Excessive heat without protection. Never heat-style without a protectant spray, and avoid temperatures above 180°C for daily use.

Frequently Asked Questions About Wig Care

How often should I wash my human hair wig?

Wash your human hair wig every 7–10 wears, or whenever product build-up becomes noticeable. Over-washing can strip natural moisture, while under-washing leads to dullness and tangling.

Can I use regular shampoo on a human hair wig?

No, you should use a sulfate-free, paraben-free shampoo specifically formulated for human hair wigs. Regular shampoos contain harsh detergents that strip moisture and shorten the lifespan of the wig.

How do I detangle a human hair wig without causing damage?

Always detangle before washing. Use a wide-tooth comb or detangling brush, starting from the ends and working upward to the roots. Apply a lightweight detangling spray for stubborn knots and avoid pulling from the top down.

Can I use heat tools on a human hair wig?

Yes, 100% human hair wigs can be heat-styled up to 200°C (392°F). Always apply a heat protectant spray first and keep temperatures at or below 180°C (350°F) for regular use. Limit heat styling to 2–3 times per week.

How should I store my human hair wig when not wearing it?

Store your wig on a mannequin head or wig stand to maintain its shape. Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. When traveling, use a satin storage bag.

How long does a human hair wig last with proper care?

With proper care and maintenance, AA WIGS units are designed to last 1–2+ years. Following a consistent washing, conditioning, and storage routine is key to maximizing longevity.
