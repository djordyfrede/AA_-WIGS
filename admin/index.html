ⓘ Dev vs. Production — Important

Your dev admin (this Replit workspace) and your live site admin (aawigs.com/admin/) each have their own separate database. Stock changes, pausing checkout, and all inventory edits you make here do not automatically apply to the live site. To control what customers see on aawigs.com, log in to aawigs.com/admin/ and make your changes there.
