Luxury Wig Length Guide

A complete comparison of 18″, 20″, 22″, 24″, and 26″ body wave wigs — where each length falls, who it suits, and how to choose the right one for you.

How Wig Length Is Measured

Wig length is always measured in a straight line from root to tip — not following the curl or wave pattern. This means a body wave wig will appear slightly shorter when worn naturally, since the waves reduce the effective hanging length.

For example, a 22-inch body wave wig measured straight will settle around the mid-back rather than falling the full 22 inches due to the wave structure. This is normal and expected — and the reason body wave always looks full and luxurious rather than flat.

18 Inch — The Polished Everyday

18″

Falls at: shoulder / collarbone

Where it falls: Just at or below the shoulder, resting around the collarbone when worn in its natural wave.

Best for: Women who prefer a clean, put-together look without managing extra length. Ideal for professional settings, active lifestyles, and first-time wig wearers who want a manageable introduction to longer hair.

Luxury recommendation: Pairs beautifully with a centre part or side sweep. The shorter length lets the HD lace hairline do the talking.

"Effortlessly polished. Easy to maintain. Perfect for daily wear."

20 Inch — The Balanced Classic

20″

Falls at: upper back

Where it falls: Upper-to-mid back, providing clear visible length without the weight of longer options.

Best for: Women who want noticeable length and body wave volume while keeping the wig comfortable and low-maintenance. The most versatile choice across all body types and face shapes.

Luxury recommendation: Excellent for high ponytails, half-up styles, and open waves. The 20-inch length moves beautifully in body wave and photographs exceptionally well.

"The perfect balance between statement and ease."

22 Inch — The Signature Length ✦

22″

Falls at: mid-back

Where it falls: Mid-back — the classic luxury wig length. This is AA WIGS' default and most popular length.

Best for: Everyone. The 22-inch body wave is the universally flattering length — long enough to command presence, manageable enough for daily wear, and the ideal canvas for body wave's natural movement.

Luxury recommendation: Our signature. If you are unsure, choose 22 inches — it is the length our brand was designed around and the one our customers return to most consistently.

"The classic choice. The one that started it all."

24 Inch — The Statement Look

24″

Falls at: lower back

Where it falls: Lower back. Long enough to turn heads; the body wave texture keeps it looking rich rather than heavy.

Best for: Women who want a glamorous, high-impact look for events, content creation, or personal style statements. Also popular with taller women who find 22 inches falls too high.

Luxury recommendation: Stunning with loose open waves and a centre part. Allow the hair to flow naturally — at 24 inches, the body wave movement is at its most dramatic.

"Glamorous but never overdone. Length with intention."

26 Inch — The Ultra Luxury

26″

Falls at: mid-waist

Where it falls: Mid-waist — the most dramatic length in the AA WIGS collection. This is a high-fashion, commanding choice.

Best for: Women who wear longer hair naturally and feel most themselves with maximum length. Content creators, stylists, and anyone who wants their hair to be the defining feature of their look.

Luxury recommendation: Requires slightly more maintenance at this length — regular detangling and proper storage on a wig stand. The payoff is a look that is undeniably exceptional.

"Maximum presence. Unmistakable luxury."

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most popular body wave wig length?

22 inches is our most popular length — it is the AA WIGS signature and suits virtually every height, face shape, and style preference.

How long is an 18 inch wig?

An 18 inch body wave wig falls approximately at the shoulder or collarbone when worn naturally. The wave pattern means it sits slightly above the straight measurement.

Is a 26 inch wig hard to maintain?

It requires more care — regular detangling, proper storage on a wig stand, and gentle washing. But many of our customers wear 26-inch wigs daily and find the maintenance straightforward with the right routine.

What wig length suits a petite frame?

18 or 20 inches tends to be proportionate for petite women, though many prefer 22 inches for a bold look. There is no wrong answer — choose the length you feel most confident in.
