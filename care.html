Protect Your Investment

Care Guide

Your wig is an investment. Here's how to keep it looking luxurious for as long as possible.

Washing Your Unit

We recommend washing your wig every 7–10 wears, or when product build-up becomes noticeable.

  1. Gently detangle with a wide-tooth comb, starting from the ends and working up.
  2. Rinse with lukewarm water — never hot. Hot water can damage the cuticle and loosen knots.
  3. Apply a sulfate-free shampoo. Work it through gently — avoid rubbing or twisting.
  4. Rinse thoroughly, then apply a moisturizing conditioner from mid-length to ends.
  5. Leave conditioner for 5–10 minutes, then rinse with cool water to seal the cuticle.
  6. Pat dry with a microfiber towel. Do not wring or twist.

Pro tip: A leave-in conditioner or argan oil on the ends helps maintain softness and shine between washes.

Styling

Our units are made with heat-friendly hair, but we recommend using heat sparingly to maximize longevity.

  1. Always apply a heat protectant before using any hot tools.
  2. Keep flat iron/curling iron temperatures at or below 350°F (180°C).
  3. Avoid applying heat directly to the lace — this can warp or damage the base.
  4. For heatless styles, try flexi rods, braids, or bantu knots overnight.

Pro tip: Finger-combing instead of brushing helps reduce shedding and preserves the body wave pattern.

Storage

Proper storage is essential to maintaining your wig's shape, softness, and longevity.

  1. Store your wig on a mannequin head or wig stand to maintain shape.
  2. When traveling, use the satin storage bag included with your order.
  3. Keep your unit away from direct sunlight to prevent color fading.
  4. Wrap or braid loosely before storing to avoid tangling.

Pro tip: A silk or satin pillowcase reduces friction while sleeping in your unit and helps maintain the style.

Lace Care

The lace is the most delicate part of your wig. Handle it with intention.

  1. When cutting lace, use sharp scissors and follow your natural hairline.
  2. If using adhesive, choose a gentle formula designed for HD lace.
  3. Remove adhesive residue with a lace-safe solvent — never pull or peel aggressively.
  4. Clean the lace area after each wear to keep it clear and re-applicable.

Longevity

With proper care, your AA WIGS unit can last 6–12 months or longer. Here are the key habits:

  1. Detangle before washing — wet tangling causes breakage.
  2. Use quality, sulfate-free hair products.
  3. Minimize heat styling.
  4. Deep condition every 2 weeks.
  5. Alternate between wigs if you have multiple units.
