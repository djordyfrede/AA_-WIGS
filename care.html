AA WIGS Care Guide

To preserve the beauty, softness, and longevity of your AA WIGS signature piece, follow these care recommendations.

Wash

Use sulfate-free shampoo and lukewarm water. Gently cleanse from roots to ends and rinse thoroughly.

Detangle

Gently detangle from the ends toward the roots using a wide-tooth comb or paddle brush.

Dry

Pat excess water with a towel. Allow the hair to air dry naturally. Avoid direct heat while drying.

Heat Styling

Use heat protectant before styling. Heat styling is recommended at moderate temperatures to preserve the integrity of the hair and lace.

Products

Use sulfate-free shampoo, moisturizing conditioner, and alcohol-free styling products to preserve softness and longevity.

Storage

Store your signature piece in its AA WIGS box when not in use.
