Lesson 2 — The Most Important Findings

This section answers one question:

"If AA WIGS already has a lot of content, what should we improve next?"

The report identifies several key findings. We'll go through them one by one.

Finding #1

AA WIGS already has enough educational content.

This might be the biggest surprise in the report.

It estimates that your Knowledge Center already contains approximately 44 unique content pages.

The report does not say:

"Write another 100 articles."

Instead, it says your next stage should be:

better organization,
higher quality,
stronger architecture.
Imagine a luxury library

Imagine you own the largest luxury library in the world.

You have amazing books.

But...

They are scattered everywhere.

No categories.

No sections.

No map.

Visitors walk in and think:

"This place has a lot... but where do I start?"

That is what Google—and many visitors—may experience.

The report is telling you:

Organize the library before buying more books.

Business Lesson

Many businesses believe:

More content = better website.

The report says:

Better structure = better website.

That's a very different philosophy.

Finding #2

The report says many of your trust articles answer almost the same question.

Examples include topics like:

buying wigs online,
identifying quality,
avoiding scams,
asking questions before buying,
overcoming fear of buying online.

Those are all valuable topics—but they're very close in search intent.

Imagine a customer

A woman searches:

"How do I avoid buying a bad wig online?"

She finds one article.

Then another.

Then another.

Then another.

After reading four pages she thinks:

"Didn't I already read this?"

Instead of becoming more confident, she becomes confused.

Google thinks similarly

Google wants each page to have one clear job.

Not five pages doing almost the same job.

The report recommends creating a cornerstone-and-spoke structure, where one main page covers the broad topic and related pages each answer a distinct question.

Think of it like this:

Buying With Confidence
        │
 ├── Avoid Wig Scams
 ├── Identify Quality
 ├── Questions Before Buying
 ├── What You'll Receive
 └── Returns Guide

Every page has its own purpose.

Finding #3

This is one of the most important lessons for a luxury brand.

The report says some of your wording is too absolute.

For example, instead of saying:

"Our wigs never shed."

You should say something that can be supported with evidence, such as:

"Each unit is inspected according to our documented quality standards before approval."

The difference is subtle, but powerful.

Luxury brands build trust by making precise, supportable claims, not exaggerated ones.

Finding #4

The report studied competitors and found that at least one major competitor already teaches customers what to expect after receiving a wig.

At first, that might sound discouraging.

But the report says it's actually validation.

It proves customers genuinely have these questions.

The opportunity for AA WIGS is to answer them better, using:

real AA WIGS photography,
clearer inspection guidance,
stronger luxury presentation,
first-party proof.

Notice something important:

The recommendation isn't to be louder.

It's to be more believable.

Finding #5

One sentence in the report saves many businesses from wasting time.

It says:

Google does not have a preferred article word count.

Many people think:

"If I write 2,000 words I'll rank higher."

The report rejects that idea.

Instead:

Write as much as the topic genuinely needs.

Sometimes that's 800 words.

Sometimes 1,500.

Sometimes less.

Quality and usefulness matter more than hitting an arbitrary number.

Finding #6 (My Favorite)

The report says something that fits AA WIGS perfectly.

It says:

Real AA WIGS photographs, inspection procedures, measurements, and customer-support data are more valuable than generic SEO copy.

This changes how you should think about content.

Many websites publish articles that anyone could write.

AA WIGS has the opportunity to publish articles that only AA WIGS can write.

For example:

Show the actual burgundy box.
Show the real inspection table.
Show how your team measures wig length.
Show your actual packaging process.
Show the exact checklist used before shipping.

Those aren't just words.

They're evidence.

Evidence is difficult for competitors to copy.

The Biggest Takeaway from Lesson 2

Phase 1 (what you've already done)
