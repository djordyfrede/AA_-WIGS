Imagine a visitor lands on:

Then what?

Many websites simply say:

The report recommends something much more intentional.

Every trust article should naturally guide the reader back to the Buying With Confidence hub or to another closely related article.

Instead of dead ends...

You create a learning journey.

Internal Linking Rules

One of the most practical parts of the report is that it gives a repeatable linking formula.

Every trust article should include:

One link back to the Buying With Confidence hub.
Two or three links to closely related trust articles.
One link to a relevant specification guide.
One link to the appropriate support page (returns, shipping, contact, etc., when relevant).
One gentle product or collection CTA after the educational question has been answered.

Notice the order.

Education comes first.

The product invitation comes last.

That's consistent with the trust-first philosophy you've been building.
