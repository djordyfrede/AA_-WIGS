The AA WIGS Luxury Packaging Experience

Every AA WIGS unit arrives inside our signature deep burgundy magnetic presentation box, lined with champagne satin for a reveal designed to feel intentional.

The Experience

Luxury Packaging Experience

Burgundy presentation box. Champagne satin interior. Designed to make every reveal feel intentional.

The Burgundy Magnetic Presentation Box

The AA WIGS outer box is constructed from rigid, deep burgundy material with a magnetic closure that opens with a satisfying, deliberate weight. The exterior carries our gold foil logo — pressed cleanly, not printed — and the edges are finished to the same standard as the product inside.

Magnetic closures are a hallmark of luxury packaging. They replace the disposable flaps of standard boxes with a mechanism that opens the same way, every time. A consistent experience. A considered reveal.

The box is also designed to be kept. Many of our customers store their wigs in the box between wears — which is exactly what it was built for.

The Champagne Satin Interior

Inside the box, every surface is lined with champagne-coloured satin. Satin is the material of choice in luxury goods for a reason: it is soft, non-abrasive, and visually rich. Against the deep burgundy exterior, the champagne interior creates an immediate contrast — a colour reveal that signals quality before the wig is even visible.

The satin lining also protects the hair. Rough or porous box linings can snag delicate strands or disrupt the wave pattern before first wear. Satin eliminates that risk entirely, ensuring your wig arrives exactly as it was prepared.

What's Inside Every AA WIGS Box

✦ Your AA WIGS Swiss HD Lace Body Wave wig in your chosen colour and length
✦ Champagne satin storage bag for between-wear protection
✦ Printed care guide card with washing and maintenance instructions
✦ AA WIGS confidence card — a personal touch, not an afterthought

Everything arrives ready to wear. No untangling, no flattened waves, no damage from transit. The packaging is engineered to protect and present.

Why Packaging Builds Trust

The packaging you receive tells you something about the company you bought from before you even touch the product. A flimsy plastic bag says: we shipped it. A rigid luxury box with satin lining says: we thought about you receiving this.

AA WIGS packaging was designed from the beginning as an extension of the wig itself. The same attention given to the lace, the hair quality, and the construction was applied to what arrives at your door.

This matters because trust in a premium product starts at the moment of delivery. When the box arrives and it feels substantial, when the magnetic closure requires intention to open, when the champagne satin interior is revealed — you know, before the wig is in your hands, that you made the right choice.

That certainty is what we build into every order.

Authenticity and Presentation

Every AA WIGS unit is quality-inspected before packaging. We check density, lace integrity, knot bleaching, pre-plucking, and overall construction. Only units that pass this check are placed into the box.

The packaging seal is the final step of that process — a physical confirmation that the product inside has been reviewed and approved before it leaves our hands.

This is not a luxury add-on. It is the standard.
