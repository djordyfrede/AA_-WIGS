How To Store A Human Hair Wig To Keep It Looking Luxury

Proper storage is the difference between a wig that lasts two years and one that lasts five months. Here is exactly how to protect your investment between every wear.

Why Storage Is Half the Care

Most wig care guides focus on washing and conditioning — but the time your wig spends off your head matters just as much as the time it is on. Improper storage causes tangling, shape loss, lace deformation, colour fading, and moisture damage, all of which accelerate the degradation of even the highest-quality human hair.

The goal of good storage is simple: maintain the wig's shape, protect its moisture balance, shield it from environmental damage, and ensure it is ready to wear in the same condition it was removed. When storage is done correctly, you save time on preparation, extend the wig's lifespan significantly, and protect a meaningful investment.

What to Absolutely Avoid

Certain storage habits are the most common causes of premature wig damage:

  • Never store a damp wig. Moisture trapped inside a bag or box creates mildew, odour, and hair damage within days. Always ensure the wig is completely dry before storage.
  • Never store in direct sunlight. UV exposure fades colour — particularly toned shades like 613 Blonde and 99J Burgundy — and dries out the hair over time.
  • Never store in a plastic bag. Plastic traps moisture and heat, creating conditions that damage both the hair and the lace.
  • Never store tangled. Always gently detangle with a wide-tooth comb from ends to roots before putting the wig away. Storing it tangled sets the tangles and makes them harder to remove later.
  • Never store in a humid bathroom. Bathroom humidity causes frizziness in the wave pattern and weakens lace over time.
  • Never pile wigs on top of each other without individual bags. Weight compresses the wave pattern and causes matting.

Step-by-Step Storage Routine

  1. Remove the wig gently. Release any clips or the adjustable band before lifting from the nape upward. Do not pull from the crown.
  2. Check that it is dry. If you wore the wig in rain or washed it recently, allow it to air dry completely on a wig stand before storing — minimum 2 hours, ideally overnight.
  3. Gently detangle. Working from ends to roots with a wide-tooth comb or your fingers, remove any tangles. Do not brush aggressively.
  4. Apply a light leave-in conditioner if needed. A small amount of leave-in spray on the mid-lengths and ends before storage keeps the hair moisturised during storage periods.
  5. Place on a wig stand or into the satin bag. For overnight storage, wig stand is ideal. For longer storage, place the wig in the satin bag gently, allowing the hair to lay loosely rather than being compressed.
  6. Store in a cool, dark, dry place. A wardrobe shelf or drawer away from sunlight and heat sources is ideal.

Travel Storage

For travel, the original AA WIGS burgundy box and satin bag provide ideal protection. The rigid box exterior prevents compression damage in luggage, while the satin bag lining protects the hair from friction. If you are travelling without the original box, a rigid hat box with a satin bag is the next best option.

Never store a wig loose in a suitcase without a protective bag. The friction against other items will create significant tangling that can be very difficult to reverse without washing.

For frequent travellers: consider a dedicated wig travel case. These rigid, compact cases are specifically designed for wig transport and are widely available online. Paired with the included satin bag, they provide excellent protection for your investment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to store a human hair wig?

For frequent wearers: on a wig stand overnight. For occasional or long-term storage: in the included satin bag inside a cool, dark, dry location. The AA WIGS original box provides additional protection for travel and long-term storage.

Can I store my wig on a Styrofoam head?

Yes. A Styrofoam wig head works for short-term overnight storage. Ensure it is secured so the wig does not fall and tangle. For display and long-term storage, a canvas or wooden mannequin head maintains shape better.

How long can you store a human hair wig without wearing it?

Indefinitely, if stored correctly. In a satin bag, away from light and humidity, a quality human hair wig can be stored for many months without deteriorating. The key is ensuring it is completely dry before storage.
