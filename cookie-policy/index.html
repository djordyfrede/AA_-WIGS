Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember information about your visit — such as your language preference or shopping cart contents — which makes your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

Cookies do not contain executable code and cannot harm your device. They cannot access other files on your computer.

How We Use Cookies

AA WIGS uses cookies for three purposes: to make the site function correctly (essential cookies), to understand how visitors use the site (analytics cookies), and to remember your preferences across visits (functional cookies). We do not use cookies for advertising or to track you across other websites.

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled. They do not collect personal information for marketing purposes.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

When you first visit AA WIGS, a cookie consent banner appears at the bottom of the screen. You can choose to:

  • Accept All — enables all cookies including analytics and functional cookies
  • Reject Non-Essential — disables analytics and functional cookies; essential cookies remain active

Your preference is stored in your browser's local storage. To change your preference, clear your browser's local storage for aawigs.com and revisit the site — the banner will reappear.

You can also disable cookies directly in your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, block, and delete cookies through their privacy or settings menu. Note that disabling all cookies may affect the functionality of the site (for example, your cart may not persist between sessions).

Third-Party Cookies

Google Analytics cookies are set by Google LLC. Google may process this data in countries outside your own. Google Analytics data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We use Google Analytics in a configuration that anonymises IP addresses before storage.

We do not use any advertising, retargeting, or social media tracking cookies.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time as our use of cookies changes or when legal requirements dictate. Any updates will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
