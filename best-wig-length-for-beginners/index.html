Best Wig Length For Beginners: 18, 20, 22, 24, or 26 Inches?

The length question stops more first-time buyers than any other. Here is a clear, practical guide to every AA WIGS length — and our honest recommendation for where to start.

The Short Answer

Start with 22 inches. It is our most popular length for a reason — and it is the default selection on the product page. At bra-strap length, 22 inches gives you the full body wave experience: visible movement, natural volume, and enough length to style in multiple ways, without the maintenance commitment of longer options.

But the right answer for you depends on your height, your lifestyle, and what look you are trying to achieve. This guide walks through every length so you can make the decision with complete information.

How AA WIGS Lengths Are Measured

All lengths are measured from root to tip on fully stretched hair. Because body wave has a natural S-wave pattern, the hair appears slightly shorter than its measured length when worn naturally — typically 1.5–2 inches shorter due to the wave compression.

A 22-inch body wave wig worn naturally will sit at a similar position as a 20-inch straight wig. Factor this in when comparing lengths.

Every Length, Explained

Length Position & Character
18 inch Sits just below the bust. Shorter, bouncier, lighter feel. Great for a chic, manageable look — but less visible wave movement than longer lengths.
20 inch At or just below the bust. Fuller than 18 inch. Good for those who prefer a medium length with good movement but less commitment than 22+.

24 inch Mid-back. Statement length — more drama, more cascade. Best for taller frames (5'4″+) or those who want impact on occasions. More maintenance required.
26 inch Lower back. Full glamour length. Striking visual impact. Heavier weight, most maintenance. Best for experienced wearers who want maximum drama.

Choosing Based on Your Height

Length looks different depending on height, because each inch of wig covers a different proportion of the body. As a general guide:

  • Under 5'2″: 18–20 inch is proportionate and flattering. 22 inch also works well. Longer lengths can overwhelm a petite frame.
  • 5'2″–5'6″ (average): 22 inch is ideal. 24 inch also works beautifully at this height.
  • 5'7″ and above: All lengths work. 24–26 inch creates the most dramatic, proportionate look on a taller frame.

These are guidelines, not rules. Personal style always overrides proportion — wear what makes you feel powerful.

Why 22 Inch Is the Best Starting Point

For beginners, the 22-inch body wave wig delivers four things that matter most when you are new to wigs:

  1. Confidence from day one. 22 inches is long enough to feel luxurious and transformative without being difficult to manage or overwhelming to style.
  2. Versatility for learning. There is enough length to experiment with different styles — half-up, ponytail, parted differently, blown out, straightened — as you develop your wig-wearing skills.
  3. Practical for daily life. Not so long that it becomes inconvenient during daily activities, commuting, or active wear.
  4. The established standard. 22 inch is what most tutorials, styling guides, and care routines are built around. Your learning resources will match your wig.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best wig length for beginners?

22 inches. It is the most popular, most versatile, and most practical starting length for first-time wig buyers — long enough for full body wave movement, manageable enough for daily wear.

Should beginners avoid long wigs?

Not necessarily. 24-inch is still manageable for motivated beginners. We recommend avoiding 26-inch as a first purchase unless you are confident with hair maintenance — the extra length adds weight and washing time that can feel daunting early on.

Does length affect how natural the wig looks?

No — the lace quality and hairline are what determine how natural the wig looks, not the length. All AA WIGS units use the same 13×6 Swiss HD lace regardless of which length you choose.

Can I cut a wig to a shorter length?

Yes — human hair wigs can be trimmed. If you want 20-inch now but might prefer 22-inch later, order 22-inch and have it trimmed. You cannot add length, but you can always remove it.
