Why We Chose Body Wave Only

By AA WIGS · Brand Story · June 2026 · 4 min read
Home › Knowledge Center › Journal › Why We Chose Body Wave Only
AA WIGS

The Signature Body Wave House

The Decision That Defined Everything

There is a moment in every brand's life when it must decide what it is. Not what it sells — what it stands for. For AA WIGS, that moment came early, and the answer was clear: we would do one thing, and we would do it at the highest possible level.

We chose body wave. Not straight. Not kinky curly. Not a catalogue of textures designed to appeal to everyone and master nothing. Body wave — one texture, one commitment, one standard of excellence applied to every single unit we offer.

To some, it might have seemed limiting. In practice, it has been the most liberating decision we ever made.

"We would do one thing, and we would do it at the highest possible level."

Why Body Wave?

Body wave is not simply a texture. It is the most versatile, universally flattering wave pattern in human hair — loose enough to feel effortless, defined enough to look intentional. It can be worn out of the box for dramatic volume, or gently diffused for something softer. It photographs beautifully in any light. It suits every face shape. It works for office hours and evening events alike.

Body wave is also the texture most customers return to, regardless of what else they try. It is, in the truest sense, the signature wave of luxury hair. Which is why we made it ours.

What Specialisation Means for You

When a brand offers everything, it optimises for nothing. Every decision becomes a compromise. The lace quality that works acceptably across ten textures will never be the ideal choice for any one of them. The density calibrated for a straight wig will feel different on a body wave unit. The sourcing standards broad enough for a large catalogue will inevitably include variance.

At AA WIGS, every decision we make — the lace grade, the density, the weft construction, the satin packaging — is optimised for one thing: the perfect body wave wig. That focus is what allows us to use 13×6 Swiss HD lace as standard. It is what allows us to offer a glueless fit that works from the first wear. It is what allows us to stand behind every single unit with complete confidence.

You are not buying a wig. You are buying the result of a brand that has thought about nothing else.
